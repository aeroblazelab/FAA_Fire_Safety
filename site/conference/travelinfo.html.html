Within Atlantic City -- Getting Around Atlantic City:
Atlantic City Jitneys run 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. For a convenient way to travel around town, everyone rides the Jitney. It's the Atlantic City Way! Jitneys travel the entire length of the city, from the inlet to Jackson Ave., from the Marina to the Casinos, servicing practically all major Atlantic City attractions. Jitney stops are located on the corner of every route and originate one block from the Boardwalk on Pacific Avenue. For your convenience, every sign located by each Casino Stop, will have color-coded numbers. Decide your route, then just look for the corresponding color-coded number on each Jitney, hop on and enjoy the ride! What is a Jitney? A Jitney is a 13 passenger mini bus. Cost is $1.50 per person. For more information call 609-344-8642.
