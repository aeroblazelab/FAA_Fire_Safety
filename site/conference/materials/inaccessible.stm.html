Materials in Inaccessible Areas

Thursday 1:30pm - 5:00pm
Papers in this session will address the flammability of materials in inaccessible areas within the aircraft fuselage. Types of materials covered will include electrical wire, ducting, foams and panels. Subjects covered will include improved materials, standards and the development of new test methods.
