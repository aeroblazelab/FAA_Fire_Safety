Thermal/Acoustic Insulation & Seat Flammability

Thursday 8:30am - 12:00pm
This session will deal with issues concerning the flammability test requirements proposed in NPRM FAA - 2000 - 7909 (Improved Flammability Standards for Thermal/Acoustic Insulation Material used in Transport Category Airplanes). The session will also include papers on aircraft seating flammability.

 

Session Chairman - Jim Peterson
