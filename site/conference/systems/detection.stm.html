Fire Detection

Tuesday 1:30pm - 5:00pm
This session will address Fire Detection within pressurized area of the aircraft. Papers will cover the development of new detection systems as well as means of certification.

 

Sessions Chairman - David Blake
Time Speaker Title of Presentation
1:30 - 2:00 Dave Blake (FAA) Aircraft Cargo Compartment Fire Detector Certification.
2:00 - 2:30 Gary Hunter (NASA) Microfabricated Chemical Sensors for Aerospace Fire Detection Applications
2:30 - 3:00 Jill Suo-Anttila (Sandia National Laboratories) Development of a Smoke Transport Model to Enhance the Certification of Fire Detection Systems in Aircraft Cargo Compartments
3:00 - 3:30 BREAK
3:30 - 4:00 Thomas Cleary (NIST) Advanced Fire Detection in Cargo Compartments
4:00 - 4:30 Bob Glaser (Walter Kidde) A Review of Aircraft Fire Detection Technology
4:30 - 5:00  Andre Freiling (DASA/Airbus) European Research on Aircraft Fire Detection
