Halon Replacement

Introduction

The Objective of this program is to determine if safe and effective substitute agents can be used to replace 1211 and 1301 in aircraft. Develop certification criteria for approval of halon replacement agents and systems.

Background

The Copenhagen Admendment to the Montreal Protocol on substances that deplete the stratospheric ozone has resulted in the cessation of halon production as of December 31, 1993. Halon is the extinguishing agent used in all U.S. production aircraft for engines, cargo compartments, lavoratories and hand-held extinguishers because of its combined fire fighting effectiveness and low toxicity
