Update

Dear International Aircraft Fire & Cabin Safety Research Community:
As you were aware, the 11th Triennial International Aircraft Fire & Cabin Safety Research Conference was tentatively scheduled for September 15-18, 2025, in Atlantic City, New Jersey. As of today, we are unable to confirm that the conference can proceed as planned. Consequently, we have made the difficult decision to postpone the September 2025 Triennial Conference and shift our planning efforts towards hosting in calendar year 2026. To ensure you receive timely updates, please take a moment to verify and update your contact information in our Fire Safety registration database by signing in and selecting Contact information from the menu on the left. This will enable us to keep you updated with the latest developments. We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to sharing more information with you as soon as possible. Thank you for your patience and understanding. We apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused.
