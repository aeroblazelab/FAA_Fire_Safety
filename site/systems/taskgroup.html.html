 Task Group on Halon Options

The Task Group on Halon Options of the International Halon Replacement Working Group (IHRWG) is reviewing and assessing halon substitution technologies for each major area of on-board aircraft use: (1) engine nacelles, (2) handheld extinguishers, (3) cargo compartments, and (4) lavatory protection. A draft of the third report being prepared by the Task Group can be found here. All manufacturers of fire suppression technologies applicable to replacement of halons on aircraft should ensure that the Task Group is informed of their products. Information on products already contained in the Draft report should be reviewed to insure accuracy. The Task Group strongly encourages the submission of recommended changes and additions; however, final decisions on information and assessments presented are solely the responsibility of the Task Group. Appendix A of the Draft Task Group report now being prepared will contain a list of manufacturers whose products are specifically discussed in the report with addresses and telephone/fax numbers. Manufacturers are requested to review this list to ensure that they are included and that the information provided is correct. Suggested changes and additions should be submitted to Robert E. Tapscott, Chairman, IHRWG Task Group on Halon Options, Email: tapscott@nmeri.unm.edu, Phone: 505-272-7252, Fax: 505-222-8230.
