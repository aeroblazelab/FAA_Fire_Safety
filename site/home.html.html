About Us
The Fire Safety Section conducts, manages and supports research programs to improve fire safety in commercial transport aircraft. The research is comprised of two separate but complimentary, programs: Fire and Cabin Safety and Fire Resistant Materials. Fire and Cabin Safety is a near term program that addresses specific aircraft applications and/or problems, whereas Fire Resistant Materials is a long range program to develop the enabling technology for ultra-fire resistant interior materials.
